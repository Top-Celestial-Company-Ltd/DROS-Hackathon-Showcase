MODULE 09 — WORKED EXAMPLE
Five claims must be proven before payment
TRUSTWORTHY AI WORKSHOP 03
Required claim Authoritative evidence Decision test
Invoice was issued Supplier invoice credential Signature valid; supplier LEI matches contract
Amount is correct Line items + tax + purchase order Recompute deterministically; currency and totals agree
Goods were accepted Buyer or warehouse receipt Object IDs, quantity, time and independence agree
Beneficiary is valid Bank or registry-backed account evidence Account is bound to the supplier legal entity

MODULE 10 — DECISION LOGIC
Hard gates first; confidence only routes review
TRUSTWORTHY AI WORKSHOP 04
HARD GATES
A failure blocks or escalates the action.
• Cryptographic proof and schema pass
• Issuer is authorized for this claim
• Credential is current at decision time
• Entity , invoice and account IDs match
• Amount recomputes; no duplicate exists
• Mandate covers operation, value and time
CONFIDENCE AND RISK
Scores decide the review path—not the facts.
• Independent corroboration strength
• Anomaly and fraud indicators
• Source quality and freshness
• Model uncertainty
• Value and jurisdictional risk
An LLM confidence score cannot override failed cryptography, authority, identity or mandate checks.

A USABLE MANDATE BINDS
• principal legal entity and authorized role
• delegate workload and deployment identity
• permitted operation and transaction class

},
"delegate": {
"spiffeId": "spiffe://buyer .example/agent/ap-17",

},
"operation": "submit_payment",
"transactionClass": "supplier_invoice",
"constraints": {
"currency": "EUR", "amountLimit": "10000.00",

},
"validFrom": "...", "validUntil": "...",

"policyVersion": "ap-policy-12",
"proof": { "type": "organization-service-signature" }

VERIFIER CHECKS
• The issuer role is authorized for delegation.
• The delegate matches the attested workload.
• The operation, amount and beneficiary are in 
scope.
• The mandate is current and not revoked.
• The nonce has not been consumed.
• The policy version is available for replay.
Illustrative JSON — production credentials require 
governed schemas, privacy controls and cryptographic 
profiles.

PILLAR 2 — WORKLOAD
Workload trust answers: what software is calling?
TRUSTWORTHY AI WORKSHOP 09
1 Build provenance source, builder , dependencies
2 Official release publisher signature + version
3 Security assessment independent evaluation
4 Deployment attestation approved image + runtime state
5 Workload identity SPIFFE ID used on this call
WHAT IT PROVES
• The running caller derives from an 
approved build.
• The deployment state was appraised 
under policy.
• The session identity names the intended 
workload.
WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE
That the invoice is correct, the goods 
arrived, or the mandate covers this 
payment.
Workload evidence binds the caller to the mandate; transaction evidence still decides whether this action is justified.

Transaction trust asks whether the facts suffice now

{
"evidenceId": "evidence:warehouse:93821",
"claimType": "GoodsReceived",
"issuer": "lei:WAREHOUSE...",
"subject": "shipment:...",
"object": "batch:...",
"assertion": { "quantity": 1024,
"condition": "accepted" },
"observedAt": "2026-08-10T13:42:00Z",
"issuedAt": "...",

"derivedFrom": ["sensor:...", "order:..."],
"credentialStatus": "https://...",
"proof": { "type": "..." }

EVALUATE FIVE QUESTIONS
1 Authentic? proof and integrity
2 Authoritative? issuer policy for this claim
3 Current? status, time and freshness
4 Corroborated? independent evidence domains
5 Permitted? mandate and transaction policy
A signature establishes attribution and integrity—not the 
truth of the underlying claim.

TRUST GRAPH — ISSUER POLICY
Every claim needs the right authoritative issuer
TRUSTWORTHY AI WORKSHOP 11
Claim Expected authoritative issuer Identity or credential link
Legal entity identity Business registry / GLEIF / vLEI issuer LEI + registry reference
Organizational role Qualified or governed role issuer vLEI OOR / role credential
Agent mandate Entity through an authorized representative principal LEI + delegate workload ID
Build provenance Build platform / software publisher artifact digest + SLSA/in-toto
Deployment state Control plane / attestation verifier deployment digest + RATS evidence
Goods received Buyer / warehouse / carrier for its own event shipment, batch, SSCC + issuer LEI
Settlement Bank or payment service payment intent + bank transaction ID
Credential format answers “how is the claim carried?” Issuer policy answers “who is allowed to say it?”

WORKSHOP SYNTHESIS
Design for verifiable action, not a truthful agent
TRUSTWORTHY AI WORKSHOP 15
Mandate
who may act
Workload
what is calling
Transaction
what is true enough
Authorized action
+ signed receipt
Build next • Use LEI and vLEI to bind mandate credentials to the real legal entity.
• Keep build, deployment, workload and transaction attestations distinct.
• Make issuer policy, hard gates, contradictions, grants and receipts machine-readable.
The objective is not to prove that an agent is truthful. It is to prove that an action followed from authenticated, authoritative and sufficiently independent 
evidence under an enforceable mandate and reproducible policy.
